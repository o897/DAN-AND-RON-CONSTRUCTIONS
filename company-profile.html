01 / Who we are

About our company

Dan and Ron Constructions (Pty) Ltd is based in Mokopane, Limpopo. We focus on structural and civil construction, steel erection and construction supplies. Our approach combines practical expertise, a commitment to quality and close collaboration with clients.
